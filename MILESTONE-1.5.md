# Milestone 1.5 - Articles and Intellectual Property Publishing System

Implemented:

- Structured article data model
- Permanent article URLs
- Individual article SEO metadata
- Article JSON-LD structured data
- Author, publication date, reading time, categories and keywords
- Related frameworks and related articles
- Branded downloadable PDF publications
- Print, share and copy-link actions
- RSS feed at /intelligence-economy/articles/feed.xml
- XML sitemap and robots.txt
- Four foundational Intelligence Economy publications

To add another article:

1. Add a new object to data/intelligenceArticles.ts.
2. Create a matching PDF in public/publications/articles/.
3. The article index, dynamic page, RSS feed and sitemap update automatically.
